Who we are

About Metrolink's

Metrolink's Group of Companies is a Nairobi-based investigations, security and debt recovery group. It brings together Metrolink's Security Services and Metrolink's Debt Collectors Ltd under one leadership, giving clients a single, trusted point of contact for sensitive matters.

Built on some fifteen years of field experience, our team gathers and analyses information, verifies facts, documents findings and traces people and property, helping clients make informed decisions and recover what is theirs.

We have worked hand in hand with the police and other investigating agencies, and we carry out every assignment with discretion, integrity and respect for the law.

How we work

Our Approach

Every assignment follows the same five stages, so clients know what to expect and every finding can be relied upon.

  1. Consultation

    We listen to your concerns, agree the objectives and set a clear scope of work.

  2. Case Assessment

    We choose the right methods and weigh the risks, resources and legal considerations.

  3. Investigation

    We gather information lawfully, through intelligence work, discreet field operations and tracing.

  4. Analysis & Verification

    We test what we find and separate verified facts from rumour.

  5. Reporting & Handover

    Findings are documented clearly and confidentially; where an offence is established, evidence goes to you and the appropriate authorities.

Confidentiality & lawful conduct

All our work is carried out in accordance with the laws of Kenya. Information is gathered by lawful means only, client details are handled in strict confidence, and our investigators never act as, or present themselves as, law enforcement officers. Matters requiring arrest or prosecution are referred to the appropriate authorities.
